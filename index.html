Photo inspection checklist

Look closely at these details. This browser-only version does not identify disease from pixels; use the checklist to collect useful evidence.

  • Photograph the top and underside of affected leaves.
  • Check stems, new growth and the soil surface.
  • Look for insects, webbing, sticky residue or spreading spots.
  • Note recent changes in watering, light and temperature.

PlantDOC offers educational guidance, not a laboratory diagnosis. Avoid applying pesticides or fertiliser until the cause is clearer.

A GOOD PLACE TO START

Three habits that help.

01

Check before watering

Feel the soil first. A fixed schedule can lead to overwatering when conditions change.

02

Give light a thought

Notice how much direct and indirect light reaches your plant throughout the day.

03

Look underneath

Inspect leaf undersides regularly; early signs of pests are easy to miss.
